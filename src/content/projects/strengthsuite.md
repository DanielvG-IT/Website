## What it is

StrengthSuite is a gym management platform designed to combine member
administration, subscriptions, access control, and training-related
functionality into a single system. The long-term vision is to connect software
directly with physical gym infrastructure such as entrance gates, QR access, and
membership validation.

## Why it was built

The project started from an interest in solving real operational challenges
faced by gyms while exploring how software can interact with physical
environments. Rather than building another generic CRUD application, the goal
was to design a platform that supports actual business workflows and secure
facility access.
