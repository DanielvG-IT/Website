## What it is

OpenCaptive is an open-source captive portal platform that allows organizations
to build customized network access experiences. It aims to provide businesses
with flexible authentication, branding, and access management capabilities
without relying on closed, vendor-locked systems.

## Why it was built

My background in networking and systems administration sparked an interest in
how users interact with network infrastructure. OpenCaptive explores the
intersection between networking, software engineering, and business operations —
a natural extension of my MBO background toward building productized software.
